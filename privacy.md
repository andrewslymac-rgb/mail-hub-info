# Mail Hub privacy policy

Mail Hub is a private email tool used only by its owner.

**What it accesses.** With the owner's permission, Mail Hub reads, organizes and sends email in the owner's own Google accounts.

**How the data is used.** Email is used only to show it to the owner and to carry out actions the owner takes, such as replying, labeling and archiving.

**Sharing.** Mail Hub does not sell, rent or share Google account data with anyone. Data is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.

**Storage.** Mail stays in the owner's Google accounts. Mail Hub keeps only the settings and sign-in tokens it needs to work, and those are stored securely.

**Removing access.** The owner can remove Mail Hub's access at any time in their Google Account settings, under third-party connections.

Mail Hub's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
